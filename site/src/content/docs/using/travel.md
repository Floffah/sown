World addresses uses the `sown://` scheme followed by its hostname:

```text
sown://luminous.floffah.dev
```

Use a world address to connect directly, or visit a public hub to discover destinations. The Capital City is a shared public hub, and communities operate their own hubs using the same world infrastructure.

## Portals

A portal will send you to another world address. The destination can belong to a different operator and has its own rules and access policies.

The client obtains the destination's world information and source, establishes a connection, and authenticates where required. You keep your persistent identity across worlds. Each operator sets the rules for accepting avatars, items, and other portable data.

A world address identifies a destination. The [discovery service](/wire/discovery/) supplies its repository revision and session endpoints.
