Your Sown identity belongs to you, independently of any world, client, hosting provider, or foundation. Your identifier, identity resolution, and Personal Data Store have separate roles:

| Concern | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Persistent identifier | Identifies the player across worlds and providers |
| Resolution | Locates signed information about that identity |
| Personal Data Store (PDS) | Hosts profile information and portable player data |

You can replace providers and resolution services. Hosting identity records does not give them ownership of the identity.

- [Identifiers and authority](/identity/authority/) explains persistent identity and signed records.
- [Personal Data Stores](/identity/personal-data/) covers provider choice and migration.
- [Verification and privacy](/identity/verification/) explains credential checks and trust boundaries.
